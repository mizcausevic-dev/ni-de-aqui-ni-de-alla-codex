Ni de Aquí Ni de Allá with Codex

Recipe attributed in the supplied brief to Danny González and Pedro Tobar at Bar Providencia, Washington, D.C. “Neither from here nor from there.” Animation by Codex for Miz. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.

  • 1 oz bourbon
  • 1 oz mezcal
  • ½ oz prepared plantain syrup (or spiced plantain purée)
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters
  • 2 dashes banana bitters
  • Fresh banana leaf, trimmed to line a rocks glass
  • One large serving ice cube; additional ice for the mixing glass

Line the rocks glass with the banana leaf; add the large ice cube. In a separate mixing glass filled with ice, combine the bourbon, mezcal, plantain syrup and both bitters. Stir until thoroughly chilled. Strain into the prepared rocks glass. The banana leaf lining is the garnish; no second leaf is added.

About the prepared syrup

The supplied method simmers fried plantains with cinnamon, water and toasted coriander, then blends and finely strains the mixture. Batch quantities and cooking times were not supplied. This film starts with prepared syrup and does not invent a syrup formula. The 30-second timeline compresses preparation for illustration; it is not a prescribed chilling time. Liquid levels are illustrative, not calibrated volumes.
